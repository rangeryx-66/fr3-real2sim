# Physics-aware drawer study: prerequisite checks did not pass

This study has not established a causal manipulation benefit from updated
physics knowledge. The changed-condition probing did not produce a usable
updated model. The registered Reactive / Stale / Updated comparison episodes
and the long-stroke extension were not executed. Their absence is not a
measured tie, a success rate, or evidence that accurate physics knowledge is
intrinsically useless.

The initial-condition M0 acquisition completed its declared FIT motions, but
the original complete held-out motion failed with persistent sliding. Its
numerical model was sealed for future independent testing; that failure was
not converted into a validation pass.

In damping condition D, the initial action and a same-World recovery with
fresh observation, a different preload, and reduced excitation both ended
in persistent sliding. A subsequent reverse-first plan passed geometric
checks but execution was rejected by the finite-chain resource admission.
No third physical grasp occurred. Release, retreat and parking were verified,
and the owned actor was closed after saving the complete evidence.

In combined condition K, the first action returned a controlled
`TCP_PROJECTION_INFEASIBLE` interruption. It released and parked, preserving
the World for fresh observation and a changed grasp and motion strategy.
The next action encountered an unavailable numerical stopping preview; the
actual exit also failed its TCP projection. The existing safety owner paused
the World and latched unconfirmed controller state with no qualified reset.
The event explicitly identifies its source as unconfirmed controller state,
not a measured force or speed hard event. The raw terminal classification
`HARD_STOP_UNPARKED`, missing exit verification, and the latch are preserved.
Owner finalization exported the evidence and released only the owned process;
process exit is not verified parking or a hardware braking result.

Both conditions retained all failed actions. Incomplete or excluded motion
was not promoted to independent validation, parameter support, or successful
contact calibration. Unsupported updated policies fell back to the same
reactive output; no repeated comparison runs were launched to manufacture an
effect. Earlier qualification successes are not A/B/C outcomes.

The remaining prerequisite is reliable probing and independently verified
stopping and exit under the existing coordinated controller. This study does
not resolve whether an accurate updated model outperforms normal feedback.
No additional controller tuning or physical experiment is authorized by this
report itself.
