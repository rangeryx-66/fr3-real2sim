# Native xArm Gripper integration

Reference: `5ced3f5`. Scope: **XARM7 / NATIVE_XARM_GRIPPER / ORACLE_HANDLE / KNOWN_MODEL / APPROXIMATE_ACTUATOR_SIMULATION**. The frozen virtual PiPER-gripper and PiPER files are not modified. This document describes integration assumptions; physical outcomes belong in the final report.

The official G1 hand is assembled directly from pinned xarm_ros `aad7e1611c9c46eb719045414394bfdd42dcb0f8`, using `xarm_gripper.urdf.xacro` and its unchanged collision/visual meshes, mass tensors, joint axes and limits. Official `link_eef` mounting has zero additional transform; `link_tcp` is 172 mm from the gripper base. `tcp_link` is a massless coordinate alias of that official frame. No virtual PiPER geometry or saved successful grasp enters native planning.

The driver joint controls five coupled joints. Isaac 6's converter emitted NewtonMimicAPI but no effective PhysX mimic constraint. The first robot-only record demonstrated 0.138485 rad coupling disagreement. Explicit PhysX mimic constraints implement the official URDF relationship, with one finite position motor and no independently driven follower motors. These are internal mechanical constraints, never constraints between the robot and object.

Four internal four-bar collision pairs are filtered: left/right finger–inner-knuckle and inner–outer-knuckle. They generated contacts at normal coupled positions and are listed in the official `xarm7_with_gripper.srdf`. Arm nonadjacent collisions, gripper/object, gripper/environment and object dynamics remain enabled and unchanged. The source files retain the exact pair list.

The arm uses the frozen sourced xArm implicit force drives, finite effort limits and gravity-equilibrium bias. The native hand uses sourced simulation stiffness 10 N·m/rad, approximate damping 0.1 N·m·s/rad, zero direct external effort command and a finite master authority capped by 30 N times the aperture Jacobian, below the URDF's much larger mechanical effort ceiling. This is an approximate simulation actuator, not hardware servo identification.

The hand adapter exposes width, position hold, release and encoder feedback. Shared logical preload requests are converted to a latched position-target offset using the approximate drive stiffness and aperture Jacobian. The offset origin is acquired once, rather than integrating closure against the current position indefinitely. The hold remains a native position command; PiPER effort-hold is not advertised as a native hardware capability. Hardware deployment needs position/preload validation and an available contact/progress sensing strategy. PhysX contact forces are simulation-only measurements.

Planner pad labels `gripper_link1/2` are logical coordinate aliases for the real left/right finger bodies. Their transformed solids are the official native meshes, not PiPER pads. Contact-patch scoring uses the real nonlinear aperture and both fingers at the same pose and aperture. The common shared grasp/base/IK planner, approach trajectory, closure-continuity repair and Pink opening policy are reused. Raw telemetry retains actual native collider identifiers and world contact points/normals/impulses.

The manufacturer's manual specifies 0–84 mm travel and 802 g mass. This pinned URDF's geometric open gap is approximately 88.924 mm and its summed hand-link mass is approximately 751.43 g. Commands are restricted to the rated 84 mm opening; official model masses are preserved rather than silently adjusted to the datasheet. This source/model discrepancy must be resolved against the purchased hardware revision before hardware deployment.

Registered before planning/physical outcomes: **45134 / original-32**, **45443 / original-38**, **45249 / original-32**. 45249 is selected from archived closed geometry and a protruding vertical handle; no IK outcome informs its selection. Each case allows at most one closed-start physical episode, with no replacement, controller tuning, release or regrasp. Planning input naming errors and failed recordings are retained separately.

Robot-only run 01 stops at recording preflight after an RTX allocation failure. Run 02 uses the corrected internal coupling and GPU/antialiasing recording environment, produces 642 decoded original frames, 5,140 physics records, 0.003758 rad maximum arm tracking error, 3.184732 mm/s maximum TCP speed, 1.537055e-5 rad maximum mimic disagreement and zero native self-contact records. It completes gravity hold, slow seven-joint motion, 80→20→80 mm physical opening/closing and final hold. This is free-space validation, not a door-opening result.
